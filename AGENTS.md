## Learned User Preferences

- BKSR must feel like a premium editorial academic research/publication institute — not lab/biotech, LMS, generic university, SaaS, ThemeForest, news-magazine, or Blogger redesign.
- Brand palette is logo-aligned navy/royal blue with warm white surfaces and sparse red accents; do not use teal/green as primary brand identity; primary CTAs stay navy/blue; sitewide and homepage page backgrounds stay pure white; the research listing section background is cream (#F7F1E6), not the cool sage/blue-gray surface.
- Prefer visual, presentation-ready pages over text-heavy brochure layouts; keep the home hero copy-light (brand, short line, CTAs—no dense paragraphs or meta strips); use a multi-photo slideshow hero rather than a single static frame; polish the accepted slideshow instead of replacing it with a wholly new composition; keep cards/components premium and custom, not generic component-library grids; where homepage cards should carry imagery (services, fields, media, notices, collaboration), use real or approved dummy images—not empty text-only boxes; keep homepage sections responsive on small devices; on small screens, simplify heavy scroll-pin/Lenis-coupled homepage animations to swipe or stacked layouts rather than broken pin motion—do not redesign approved desktop section compositions when fixing mobile; when asked for a small fix (e.g. spacing or a View control), restore the prior approved design rather than redesigning the whole card or section; size cards/frames to their content (no empty vertical stretch from fixed media min-heights); homepage section subtitles are selective—keep meaningful supporting lines (e.g. Our Programs, Focus Areas, Collaboration, BKSR in Media, Who we are) and omit redundant ones under self-explanatory titles (Meet our team, Message from Executive, From the library, Notices & Events, Blog); listing hubs (`/notices`, `/events`, `/news-events`) should use homepage navy rounded-card language (featured + grid + filter chips)—not thin divider lists or mismatched layouts; `/events` should feel like a program calendar (date stamps, featured gathering, upcoming/archive); keep hub intro copy light and avoid over-decorated chrome (section bands, spines, jump-chip atlases) when separate navy cards already communicate hierarchy; inner pages must match the homepage design language via full-bleed photo + deep navy `PageHero` (not flat/pale heroes)—layout-only polish is not enough if heroes still feel off-brand; do not put arrow sibling sub-navigation strips (Ongoing / Completed / Research areas / Grants and similar) on page heroes—header dropdowns already cover section nav; Contact email/call and a resource’s external open link are not that sub-nav; sitewide buttons follow the homepage pill style (`rounded-full`), not square corners; homepage Blog hub “View all blogs” is a navy pill Button matching View all talks (not a text link), and Blog section padding matches Notices & Events tightness (not huge default Section py); keep a shared pre-footer CTA band (Start a conversation + Contact + Apply to join) on every public page: navy Matter.js ball-pit badges that must never cover/hide the CTAs; use solid fill on the secondary CTA (outline/transparent fails when balls settle behind it); on mobile keep a short ball floor without a large empty navy void between buttons and badges; preserve vertical page scroll (axis-aware touch); site footer should match homepage theme with creative layout/illustration—not a generic link dump—while keeping link/contact copy readable over the art (frosted raft / paper wash, not tiny or low-opacity type); omit placeholder taglines under the footer logo until authored; keep footer nav aligned on small screens; highlight social icons (Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube).
- Homepage services/what-we-do uses CSS sticky stacking panels (Figma look + sticky-stack concept): next card rises over the previous while titles stay visible (not a full cover); use the Figma two-tone treatment and card shape; motion must stay smooth without lag, broken geometry, or sudden mid-scroll scale jumps.
- Backend/CMS is in scope: Next.js Route Handlers + MongoDB (lean related collections, avoid scatter) + Cloudinary for image/file storage with public CDN URLs stored on media docs (not R2); keep public reads fast (cache/revalidate); default demo may stay local until Mongo env is set; People claim remains phased—hardened custom JWT/OTP (not Clerk) after CMS content APIs; admin people add is invite-lite (email + name + role/sector only—user completes profile after account create; new roster rows append at end); admin/CMS UI must stay non-developer-friendly (plain-language overview/sidebar labels such as Welcome banner / Banner buttons, not opaque Hero/CTA jargon) and manage real content operations (publications, research, events, people, ED message, registration/join forms)—not SEO fields, button colors, or other design chrome; Research is managed at `/admin/research` (category-wise cards with external journal/DOI links—no internal research detail pages for now).
- When aligning to a stakeholder layout diagram or Figma Home frame, match primary nav labels/routes and homepage section inventory and composition; adapt references to BKSR tokens and content rather than pasting exports; apply the Figma title/display font styling site-wide; place Message from Executive Director below the hero when that section is in scope; keep documented Collaboration on record with hover-to-reveal behavior; homepage Who we are + How we work (sticky identity left, scroll-synced step timeline right on desktop; stacked on mobile) sits before the ED message and maps to diagram item “Shaping Evidence-Based Policy…”; Who we are / What we do CTAs should connect to the About hub and its subpages rather than dead or mismatched routes.
- Meet our team: one centered featured Executive Director card above four category cards, same card shape for featured and category cards; member cards show name/role on the front and flip in-place on hover to a bio back; include a View full team CTA to `/people`; demo names/roles/bios and stock portraits are OK until a real roster is supplied—still do not invent partners, testimonials, or affiliations; People category hubs (`/people/research-team`, etc.) must use that same flip-card directory language as `/people`, not a separate static/CMS list template—except `/people/executive-director` with a single ED, which uses portrait + homepage director message + View profile (not a one-card flip hub); avoid stacking a duplicate navy Apply to join CTA on `/people` when the sitewide Start a conversation pre-footer already covers it; `/people` directory and person profile pages are approved—do not prioritize redesigning them when hubs are the gap.
- Homepage Focus Areas uses a scroll-pinned left-to-right card reveal: pin until one extra card enters after the visible row fills (first card exits), then unpin and reveal the pager; cards need a clear View affordance; the pager must work as soon as it appears and match the Figma control design.
- What our researchers say: change scroll/animation only—do not redesign cards; use a scroll-pinned horizontal inchworm/push chain (trailing card nudges the prior forward; gaps compress on push but never stack/overlap); image+quote pairs should advance together as one unit; after about two scroll-driven advances prefer a control for further cards rather than a long pin that traps scrolling; match `docs/2026-09-10 19-14-56.mp4` / latest reference videos; keep motion smooth, not jerky or all-cards-moving-as-one.
- Make strong design decisions from the repo, legacy site, and specs; take inspiration only (do not copy) from Stanford HAI, ODI, IIED, Altos Labs, and local peers like rursbd.org, dursbd.com, jnurhss.org, curhs.org, and bdresearchsociety.com; when stakeholder Word docs are supplied for Research, Publications, Activities, or BKSR in Media, take content exact same-to-same (no paraphrase); avoid repetitive clarifying questions when the answer is already available.
- Site header must be one universal shared component site-wide (not per-page variants): floating glass/transparent-blur island (not a full-width solid white bar); use a crisp full-colour BKSR lockup including the “BK School of Research” wordmark (not a cropped mark) on light/warm surfaces (no forced white plate) and the white logo variant on deep navy/blue; the official lockup source is `docs/logo/WhatsApp Image 2026-09-17 at 8.02.24 PM.jpeg`, served as transparent brand PNGs in `public/brand`; keep the lockup readable on small devices; nav dropdowns/drawers use deep navy glass matching homepage cards (not flat white SaaS panels); on desktop, hover opens child drawer, parent label click navigates to the base hub route, sub-item click navigates to sub-routes (mobile stays accordion); use Lenis for site-wide smooth scrolling.
- Hero H1 should read "BK School of Research" on a single line (do not break the brand name across lines); do not use a leading red eyebrow hairline/rule before section labels site-wide; marquee/ticker lines stay single-line and loop continuously.

- Design and freeze audits are tracked in `docs/route-audit.md`, `docs/content-migration-report.md`, and `docs/final-design-audit.md`.
